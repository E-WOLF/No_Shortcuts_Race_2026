How handoffs work

City streets, Santa Monica to Compton

  • The van drives the race route and waits at the next handoff.
  • Anything before 7 AM is in the dark. Headlamp and reflective vest on.
  • Dougie runs one 8 mile block in round 1, after sunrise. The van leapfrogs him with water.

LA River trail

  • The runner always carries a phone on the trail.
  • The path is on the east bank from Rosecrans to Long Beach. No streets to cross and no van access between the bridges.
  • The van drives ahead, sends the incoming runner onto the trail and collects the outgoing runner where the trail meets the road.
  • Mid-trail handoff is the bike path entrance on Del Amo Blvd. Van parks on Oregon Ave, first right after the bridge heading east.
  • Follow the trail south until it passes under PCH. Meet the van at the first bike path exit after, by Ocean Blue Environmental.
  • Trail reviews report encampments south of Willow St. That is the last mile of the second trail leg, in daylight.
  • These two legs stay longer than the rest because the van can only reach the trail at the bridges.

Coast Highway, Long Beach to San Clemente

  • The road narrows in places. Be careful and run the bike path where there is one.
  • Careful at the traffic circle in Long Beach.
  • Where the van cannot pull over, it drops and collects runners the same way as on the river trail.

The I-5 drive from San Mateo Point

  • Nobody runs on a freeway. Camp Pendleton means everyone rides, runner included.
  • Team photo at San Mateo Point, then I-5 south to Exit 54C.
  • Cross the road after the exit and pull into the Chevron. The next runner picks up Coast Highway there.

Oceanside to the finish

  • All of it is after sunset at this pace. Lights and vests on for every leg.
  • Legs get shorter each round, so handoffs come every 10 to 20 minutes. The next runner should be ready before the van stops.
  • The van follows the race route. No stopping on the Torrey Pines grade or on Torrey Pines Rd.

Everyone brings

  • Headlamp and a reflective or light-up vest.
  • Race kit, tank and hat, worn at the start.
  • Phone, charged. Tile tracker for race tracking.
  • Team kit in the van: first aid kit and pepper spray.

Times start from a 5:00 AM gun and use each runner's pace from the table at the top, 9:30 until someone changes it. Once a leg has a logged finish time, the next runner starts from it and every later time moves. The I-5 stop is planned at 40 minutes until you log the restart. Times not yet logged do not include handoff time.

Handoffs sit on the Strava route at spots taken from the organizers' turn sheet and bathroom map. Stops tagged Confirm stop have no verified landmark, so check them on Street View before race day. Confirm hours for any business bathroom.
